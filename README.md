A 24/7 AI shopping assistant for fashion boutiques that answers customer questions, recommends products using live inventory, and recovers sales after business hours. ### Enterprise Automated Revenue Reconciliation for Multi-Gateway SaaS

An automated financial reconciliation system for a global SaaS business, integrating multiple payment gateways with accounting workflows to normalize, validate, and reconcile subscription revenue. Built with Stripe, PayPal, Paddle, n8n, and QuickBooks. It cut month-end reconciliation from 4–5 business days to under 10 minutes and eliminated duplicate ledger entries.
