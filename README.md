## Selected Work

### Synacuity | Multi-Tenant AI SaaS for QSR Operations

Built and deployed a production multi-tenant platform supporting 3,700+ KFC U.S. locations and 102 KFC Pakistan locations.

The system ingests Google Business Profile reviews at scale, resolves tenant and location context, converts unstructured guest feedback into structured AI intelligence and operational KPIs, and manages AI-assisted response workflows through direct Google Business Profile publishing.

The production architecture spans event-driven ingestion, asynchronous processing, PostgreSQL/Supabase data models, Claude-based intelligence pipelines, multi-tenant routing, human-in-the-loop workflows, CI/CD, monitoring, retry and recovery logic, usage tracking, and AI cost optimization.

→ [Technical Showcase](https://github.com/hamid-xcerlabs/synacuity-showcase)  
→ [Case Study](https://hamid.xcerlabs.com/case-studies/kfc)  
→ [Live Product](https://app.synacuity.com)

### AI Shopping Concierge for Fashion Retail - AI-Powered Customer Experience

An AI-powered commerce system connecting conversational channels with live Shopify inventory, semantic product retrieval, personalized recommendations, automated follow-up workflows, and human escalation.

It contributed to a 32% higher conversion rate, a 27% increase in average order value, and 70%+ of customer queries answered automatically.

→ [Case Study](https://hamid.xcerlabs.com/case-studies/ai-commerce-intelligence-system)

### Enterprise Automated Revenue Reconciliation for Multi-Gateway SaaS

An automated financial reconciliation system for a global SaaS business, integrating Stripe, PayPal, Paddle, CRM, and accounting workflows to normalize, validate, reconcile, and report subscription revenue.

Built around automated transaction processing, discrepancy detection, exception handling, currency normalization, accounting synchronization, and operational reporting.

The system reduced month-end reconciliation from 4–5 business days to under 10 minutes and eliminated duplicate ledger entries.

→ [Case Study](https://hamid.xcerlabs.com/case-studies/saas)
